# Voice options

Checked 2026-11-23 against the Ultravox voices overview, https://docs.ultravox.ai/voices/overview.md

- VERIFIED: Ultravox offers built in voices, voice cloning, and a way to bring your own voice from another provider (external voice).
- UNVERIFIED: which external voice providers work, and their limits. ElevenLabs is named in the Ultravox docs as an option. Using it needs your own ElevenLabs key and its own terms.
- UNVERIFIED: that a self hosted cloning service can be used through the generic external voice option. Treat it as an idea to try, not a promise.
- Nobody who built Ringlark can hear a voice. See docs/VOICE-OPTIONS.md for how to choose by listening.
